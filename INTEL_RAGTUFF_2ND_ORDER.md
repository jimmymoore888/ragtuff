## II. AI Treasury Architecture

Current Industry Model

Most AI treasury systems follow this structure:

AI → Strategy → Execution → Capital

Constraints are soft:
	•	governance override
	•	emergency intervention
	•	supply modification

This is structurally unstable at scale.

⸻

RagTuff Model

RagTuff reorders the stack:

Invariant Law → AI → Strategy → Execution → Capital

Key properties:
	1.	AI cannot mint beyond cap.
	2.	AI cannot bypass burn ceilings.
	3.	AI cannot accelerate unlock schedules.
	4.	AI cannot alter treasury distribution logic.
	5.	AI cannot rewrite constitutional modules.

AI becomes a bounded optimizer.

Not a sovereign allocator.
